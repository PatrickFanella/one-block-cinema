## Current local creator

One-Block Cinema can be used without an account. Draft project metadata and source media are stored in the app's private container on the device. The current creator does not upload drafts, collect analytics, serve advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

Camera, microphone, and photo-library access are requested contextually for recording or importing media. The app checks available disk space before export. Export and sharing occur only after the user asks for them and selects a destination through the system share sheet.

Deleting a project removes its app-owned media and local metadata. Deleting the app removes its app container, subject to the device platform's backup and deletion behavior.

The Settings screen can create a human-readable JSON export of local project metadata, shot prompts, edit decisions, and preserved recovery issues. The export excludes source video and audio bytes and strips private app-container paths. The user chooses where to send or save the resulting file through the system share sheet.

## Connected features

Accounts, cloud sync, community publishing, notifications, analytics, and AI critique are disabled in the current release configuration. Before any is enabled, this policy will identify the data collected, purpose, recipients/processors, retention period, deletion process, age rules, and applicable choices. Existing local projects will not be uploaded merely because a user signs in; each project requires explicit sync intent.

Public publishing will require a 13+ account, an explicit visibility choice, safety review, and user controls for deletion, reporting, and blocking. AI critique will require separate per-film consent and will use only the exported film's transcript, technical metadata, and a capped frame sample—not unused source clips.

## Security and rights

We use access controls, private-by-default storage, encrypted transport for connected features, and operational review appropriate to the sensitivity of user-created media. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information and to appeal certain moderation decisions. Requests may be sent to `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

## Children

The offline creator is available without an account. Accounts and community features are intended only for people age 13 or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. Contact `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` about a suspected underage account.
