# Legal and privacy release artifacts

These documents describe the current local-only build and the policy changes required before connected features are enabled.

- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` — public-facing policy draft.
- `TERMS.md` — public-facing terms draft.
- `SUPPORT.md` — support and privacy contact requirements.
- `RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md` — deletion and evidence-retention rules.
- `APP_STORE_PRIVACY.md` — App Store disclosure worksheet.

Legal counsel and the product owner must replace every bracketed placeholder and approve these documents before public release. Enabling accounts, sync, community, analytics, notifications, Mux, or AI requires a fresh review of the privacy manifest, App Store disclosures, policy text, and consent UX.
